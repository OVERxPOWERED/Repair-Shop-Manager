Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

FixPro ("we", "our", or "us") provides a multi-tenant business management application for mobile, computer, and electronics repair shops in India. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect your information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable mobile app store policies.

1. Information We Collect

To provide repair management services, FixPro collects the following information:

  • Technician & Store Account Data: Mobile phone numbers for secure OTP authentication, staff names, assigned roles, and shop profile details (shop name, business address, GSTIN, UPI ID).
  • Repair Intake & Device Data: Device brand, model, customer-reported faults, diagnostic notes, condition tags, photographic evidence of device physical condition, and device identifiers (IMEI / Serial numbers) for ownership verification.
  • Customer Contact Details: Customer name, phone number, and delivery address recorded by shop owners for job sheet updates and invoicing.
  • Billing & Payment Records: Tax invoices, advance receipts, payment mode (cash / UPI / card), and transaction timestamps.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process personal and business data solely for:

  • Providing core repair tracking, job sheet generation, and inventory management.
  • Sending automated transaction notifications (SMS/WhatsApp) regarding repair status changes.
  • Generating GST-compliant invoices, credit notes, and thermal print receipts.
  • Securing user sessions via device authorization and cryptographic token verification.

3. Data Retention & Statutory Preservations

In accordance with Indian commercial, tax, and GST laws:

  • Tax Invoices & Financial Records: Statutory books of account and invoices must be retained for at least 6 years (72 months). These records are stored in immutable snapshots.
  • Temporary Data: OTP challenges expire within 5 minutes and are purged within 7 days.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and mobile store policies, you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate account deletion directly from the FixPro app under More → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Public Web Deletion: You may also request account deletion at any time via our public portal at https://fixpro.in/account/delete/ without having the app installed.
  • Grace Period: Account deletion includes a 7-day grace period during which you can cancel the request by signing back into your account. Upon expiration, your personal phone number and name are irreversibly anonymized.

5. Security Safeguards

All sensitive credentials and session tokens are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 / Fernet key encryption. Hardware-backed Keystore / Keychain storage is enforced on mobile native builds.

6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, grievance redressal, or data erasure requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@fixpro.in.
